Save Him – Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE, e.g. 9 October 2026]

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Save Him (the "Game") collects, how it is used, and what choices you have. The Game is published by Elzbieta Wilson ("we", "us"), based in Poland.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller of the data described in this policy is Elzbieta Wilson. You can contact us at poprzecznadev@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

We keep data collection deliberately minimal. The Game does not require an account or login, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

The Game collects anonymous gameplay analytics, linked only to a randomly generated anonymous player ID stored on your device:

  • Game started – that a run began, which version of the Game you are playing (for example standard, kids or hardcore), the app build number, and how many runs you have played.
  • Game over – your score, your best score so far, and what ended the run (for example a wrong tool or being too slow, and which obstacle it was).
  • Milestones – when you reach certain scores in the Game.

Our analytics provider may also receive technical information that is sent automatically when an app connects to the internet, such as your IP address, device or operating system type, and approximate location derived from the IP address (for example country).

The Game also stores some information locally on your device, such as your best score and game progress. This stays on your device and is not sent to us unless it is part of the analytics described above.

3. Why we use it

We use this information only to understand how people play, to find out where the Game is too hard or confusing, to measure whether players come back, and to improve the Game. We do not use it to identify you, and we do not sell your data.

4. Legal basis (EU/EEA users)

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we process this data based on our legitimate interest in improving and maintaining the Game (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Who receives the data

We use PostHog as our analytics service provider. PostHog processes the analytics data on our behalf. Data is stored on PostHog servers located in EU. You can read PostHog's own privacy information at posthog.com/privacy.

We do not share your data with advertisers or sell it to anyone. If we add advertising in the future, we will update this policy to describe the advertising provider and what data it receives.

6. How long we keep it

We keep analytics data for as long as it is useful for improving the Game, and no longer than 24 months unless the law requires otherwise.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to a supervisory authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), uodo.gov.pl.

The Game does not ask for your name, email address or any account details. The player ID it uses is a random number stored only on your device, and we have no way of linking that ID to you, your device or its owner. For this reason we are not able to identify which analytics data relates to a particular person or child, and we are therefore not able to respond to individual requests for access, correction or deletion of that data.

You remain free to contact us at the email above with any questions or concerns about this policy, and you have the right to complain to a supervisory authority (see above).

You can stop all future data collection by uninstalling the Game, which removes the player ID from your device. If you install the Game again, it creates a new random ID that is not connected to the previous one.

8. Children

Save Him includes a kids version of the Game, and children may play it. In all versions, including the kids version, the Game collects only the anonymous gameplay analytics described in section 2, linked to a random player ID stored on the device. We do not collect names, email addresses, precise location, advertising identifiers or other personal information from children, and we do not show personalised advertising.

Because we cannot otherwise tell which data belongs to which child, parents and guardians who want to see or delete the data linked to their child's device can find the player ID in the Game on death screen in Kids Mode and email it to poprzecznadev@gmail.com. We will delete the data linked to that ID within [30 days]. Uninstalling the Game also removes the ID from the device.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the data we process. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this address, with the date of the last update shown at the top.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or about your data, email us at poprzecznadev@gmail.com.
