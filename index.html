2. What we collect

We keep data collection deliberately minimal. The Game does not require an account or login, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

The Game collects anonymous gameplay analytics, linked only to a randomly generated anonymous player ID stored on your device:

  • Game started – that a run began, which version of the Game you are playing (for example standard, kids or hardcore), the app build number, and how many runs you have played.
  • Game over – your score, your best score so far, and what ended the run (for example a wrong tool or being too slow, and which obstacle it was).
  • Milestones – when you reach certain scores in the Game.

Our analytics provider may also receive technical information that is sent automatically when an app connects to the internet, such as your IP address, device or operating system type, and approximate location derived from the IP address (for example country).
